# FPS 1% Low — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Show the 1% low FPS as its own group (`FPS 144 | 1% 98 | CPU …`) in the Steam-like bar, behind a new `show_low` config key.

**Architecture:** `FpsCounter` (ETW thread, pure) records frame gaps in a preallocated ring and returns the 99th-percentile frame time as FPS with each 1 s sample; `etw::SHARED` carries it as `low_bits` under the existing `sample_qpc` Release/Acquire commit; `App::tick` copies it into `Stats.fps_low`; `layout::build` draws it.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 1.100, edition 2024, `windows` 0.62.2 (no new features, no new crates).

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-01-steam-overlay-port-design.md` — section *1% low*, Steam group 1b, `show_low` config row.

## Global Constraints
- AGENTS.md is binding (two threads, ETW callback atomics only, no allocation in the callback, `panic = "abort"`).
- Definition: nearest-rank p99 frame time, ascending index `ceil(0.99·n) − 1`, over the last 10 s of gaps; `n < 100` → `None`.
- Ring capacity `LOW_CAP = 4096` `u32` gaps + 4096 `u32` scratch, allocated in `FpsCounter::new`.
- Gaps > `2·freq` are not recorded. One source only: highest priority seen since reset (D3D9 > DXGI > Kernel); a higher one clears the ring.
- `show_low` default `0`; FPS-Only layout never shows it.
- Budgets unchanged: exe ≤ 550 KB, in game < 0.5% of one core, ≤ 10 MB.
- `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy --release --all-targets -- -D warnings`, `cargo test` clean before each commit. Run cargo with `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=4` and `RUST_TEST_THREADS=2`.

## Review Focus
1. Mixed sources (Vulkan game: kernel events first, then DXGI appears) ⇒ low uses DXGI gaps only, no kernel/DXGI interleaving that would halve gaps (Task 1 `low_source_priority`).
2. Alt-tab or pause ⇒ the multi-second gap never becomes the 1% low (Task 1 `low_ignores_pause_gap`).
3. Game switch ⇒ the new game never shows the old game's low (Task 1 `reset_clears_low`; Task 3: low read under the same `sample_pid` and freshness as FPS).
4. Above 400 fps ⇒ the ring holds < 10 s; result still valid, over a shorter window (Task 1 `low_ring_wraps`).
5. Existing `config.ini` without `show_low` ⇒ loads with `show_low=false`, nothing else changes (Task 2 parse test).

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `src/fps.rs` `mod tests` (freq 1000, ticks = ms). Existing tests switch to `.map(|s| s.fps)` where they compare FPS. New tests:
  - `low_none_below_100_frames`: 60 DXGI frames evenly over 1 s ⇒ sample `Some`, `low == None`.
  - `low_steady_100fps`: DXGI every 10 ms for 2 s ⇒ last sample `low ≈ 100` (±0.5).
  - `low_is_p99_gap`: 301 DXGI frames (300 gaps), gap 10 ms except the last 4 gaps of 50 ms ⇒ `low ≈ 20` (index `ceil(297) − 1 = 296` lands on a 50 ms gap); with only 3 spikes ⇒ `low ≈ 100`.
  - `low_window_10s`: 5 s at 20 fps, then 11 s at 100 fps ⇒ last `low ≈ 100`.
  - `low_source_priority`: Kernel every 5 ms and DXGI every 10 ms over 2 s ⇒ `low ≈ 100` (DXGI only); Kernel alone at 200 fps for 1.5 s then DXGI starts ⇒ samples after DXGI take over report `None` until 100 DXGI gaps, then `≈ 100`.
  - `low_ring_wraps`: 1000 fps for 6 s (6000 frames > `LOW_CAP`) ⇒ `low ≈ 1000`, no panic.
  - `reset_clears_low`: 2 s at 100 fps (low `Some`), `reset(same target, now)`, then 1 s at 50 fps ⇒ next sample `low == None` (49 gaps < 100, nothing from before the reset).

- [ ] **Step 2: Run** `cargo test fps::tests` ⇒ FAIL (no `Sample`/`low`).

- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** in `src/fps.rs`:
  - New fields: `gaps: Box<[u32]>`, `scratch: Box<[u32]>` (both `vec![0; LOW_CAP].into_boxed_slice()` in `new`), `head: usize`, `len: usize`, `last: i64`, `low_src: Option<Source>`.
  - `Source` gets a private `rank()`: D3d9 2, Dxgi 1, Kernel 0.
  - In `on_present` (after the pid/target checks, before the window maths): if `low_src` is `None` or `src` ranks higher ⇒ `low_src = Some(src)`, `len = 0`, `last = now`. Equal ⇒ `gap = now - last; last = now;` push `gap as u32` when `0 < gap <= 2·freq`. Lower ⇒ ignore.
  - When the window closes and a sample is returned: walk back from the newest gap summing as `u64` until the sum exceeds `10·freq` or `len` is exhausted; `n` = gaps taken; `n < 100` ⇒ `None`; else copy into `scratch[..n]`, `select_nth_unstable((99·n).div_ceil(100) - 1)`, `low = freq as f32 / gap as f32`.
  - `reset` also clears `len`, `low_src`.
  - `///` on the ring: why `u32` (≤ 2 s at 10 MHz QPC fits) and the `ponytail:` from the spec (> ~400 fps covers < 10 s; raise `LOW_CAP`).

- [ ] **Step 4: Run** `cargo test fps::tests` ⇒ PASS; `cargo build` ⇒ OK.
